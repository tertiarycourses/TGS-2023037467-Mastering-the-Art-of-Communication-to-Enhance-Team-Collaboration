## About the course

The course preserves the three official domains of the approved programme and rebuilds them as realistic workplace and customer scenarios. Detailed procedures, role cards, questions, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria are in the Learner Guide and the individual activity folders rather than on the slides.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Evaluate workplace communications practices for the organisation.
- **LO2:** Develop a communications plan to implement communication strategies.
- **LO3:** Identify solutions to address gaps and barriers in workplace communications.

## Topics covered

1. Evaluate Workplace Communications Practices
2. Develop a Communications Plan
3. Address Communication Gaps and Barriers
