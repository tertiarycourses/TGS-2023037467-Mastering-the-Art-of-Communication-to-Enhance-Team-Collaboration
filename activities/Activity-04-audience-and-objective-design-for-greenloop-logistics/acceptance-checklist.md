## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Uses provided evidence rather than stereotypes
- [ ] Objectives contain audience, behaviour, measure, and deadline
- [ ] Segments reflect differing stakes
- [ ] Messengers and proof match audience needs

## Closed-loop checks

- [ ] Facts are separated from assumptions.
- [ ] The audience need and desired behaviour are explicit.
- [ ] Channel choice fits urgency, ambiguity, reach, and record.
- [ ] Message includes action, owner, timing, and escalation.
- [ ] Receiver understanding is confirmed in their own words.
- [ ] Evidence is complete and suitable for assessor review.
