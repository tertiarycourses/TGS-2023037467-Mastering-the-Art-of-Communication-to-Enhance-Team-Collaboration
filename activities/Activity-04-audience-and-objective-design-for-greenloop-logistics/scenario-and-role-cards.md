## Scenario

GreenLoop Logistics is introducing route-optimisation software that changes driver schedules and customer delivery windows. Union representatives worry about workload, dispatchers fear losing autonomy, drivers want practical support, and key customers want reliability. Engagement data shows lower trust among night-shift employees and higher mobile usage than email readership.
