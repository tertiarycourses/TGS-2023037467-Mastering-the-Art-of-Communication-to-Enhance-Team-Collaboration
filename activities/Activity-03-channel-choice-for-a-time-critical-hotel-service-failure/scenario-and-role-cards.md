## Scenario

At Meridian Bay Hotel, a water-pressure problem affects 28 rooms during a sold-out weekend. Engineering needs 45 minutes to diagnose it. Front Office must brief guests, Housekeeping needs room-impact data, and the General Manager wants a record of decisions. Previous incidents produced conflicting chat messages and duplicate compensation offers.
