## Scenario

Northstar Mobility launches an accessible on-demand transport service. A pilot receives strong demand but also driver confusion, inconsistent customer updates, and poor escalation of accessibility needs. Leaders need an evidence-based benchmark, an engagement-aware communication plan, and a coaching solution before the city-wide launch.
