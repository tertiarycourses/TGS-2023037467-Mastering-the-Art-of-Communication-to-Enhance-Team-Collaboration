# Activity 05 - Build the HarborCare Communications Plan

**Course:** Mastering the Art of Communication to Enhance Team Collaboration and Customer Satisfaction

**Alignment:** K2, K3, K4, A2, A3  
**Version:** v10.1 | 17 August 2026

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] All plan fields have owners
- [ ] Every message links to a behaviour objective
- [ ] Listening moments appear before and after launch
- [ ] Metrics cover reach, understanding, action, and trust

## Closed-loop checks

- [ ] Facts are separated from assumptions.
- [ ] The audience need and desired behaviour are explicit.
- [ ] Channel choice fits urgency, ambiguity, reach, and record.
- [ ] Message includes action, owner, timing, and escalation.
- [ ] Receiver understanding is confirmed in their own words.
- [ ] Evidence is complete and suitable for assessor review.
