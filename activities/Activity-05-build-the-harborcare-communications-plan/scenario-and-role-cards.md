## Scenario

HarborCare Home Services is consolidating two service teams while launching a same-day customer callback standard. Employees need clarity on roles; supervisors need coaching tools; customers need confidence that continuity will improve. The launch is six weeks away, and previous changes suffered from announcement-heavy communication with little listening.
