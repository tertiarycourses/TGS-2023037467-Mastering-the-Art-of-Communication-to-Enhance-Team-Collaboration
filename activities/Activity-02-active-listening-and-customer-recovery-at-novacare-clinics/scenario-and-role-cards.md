## Scenario

A patient arrives for a specialist appointment that was moved by the call centre but not updated in the clinic schedule. The patient has taken unpaid leave and is visibly frustrated. The receptionist has a full queue, the nurse knows a slot may open, and the duty manager must restore trust without promising what the clinic cannot deliver.

## Scenario questions

1. What did the customer need emotionally and practically?
2. Which phrase demonstrated understanding without admitting an unverified fault?
3. Where did nonverbal behaviour support or contradict the words?
4. What follow-up would close the loop?
