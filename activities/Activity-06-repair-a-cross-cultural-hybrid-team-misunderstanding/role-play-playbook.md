# Activity 06 - Repair a Cross-Cultural Hybrid-Team Misunderstanding

**Course:** Mastering the Art of Communication to Enhance Team Collaboration and Customer Satisfaction  
**Course Code:** TGS-2023037467  
**Alignment:** K3, K5, A3  
**Version:** v10.0 | 16 August 2026

## Detailed role-play playbook

### Preparation - 10 minutes

1. Read the scenario and your confidential role card.
2. Mark facts, assumptions, emotions, interests, and non-negotiable constraints.
3. Define the shared purpose and the communication outcome you want.
4. Choose the first phrase you will use and the evidence you will listen for.

### Round 1 - baseline

1. Identify possible language, perception, culture, channel, and emotional barriers.
2. Prepare a shared-purpose opening and neutral observation.
3. Facilitate each perspective using paraphrase and clarification.
4. Separate facts, interpretations, and commitments.
5. Agree decision language, confirmation protocol, and escalation rule.
6. Run a follow-up check for restored participation and trust.

### Observer coaching - 7 minutes

- Record exact language and observable behaviour, not personality judgements.
- Identify one strength, one meaning gap, and one alternative phrase.
- Check clarity, listening, tone, channel, ownership, and follow-up.

### Round 2 - deliberate practice

1. Repeat the decision moment with the coached alternative.
2. Confirm shared meaning, action, owner, timing, and escalation.
3. End only when every role can state the same decision in their own words.

### Debrief

1. Which meaning was assumed rather than confirmed?
2. How can the facilitator stay curious without stereotyping?
3. What shared protocol prevents recurrence?
4. How will quieter team members be invited back into dialogue?
