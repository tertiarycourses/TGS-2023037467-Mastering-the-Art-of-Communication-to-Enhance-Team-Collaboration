## Scenario

EverBright Electronics assembles smart-home devices in Singapore. Sales promises customised delivery dates, Operations works from a weekly spreadsheet, and Customer Care learns of delays only after customers call. Employee pulse comments show that junior staff hesitate to challenge optimistic deadlines. Three enterprise customers have escalated late orders in the last month.
